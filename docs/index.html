Purpose

Not insurance sold to communities. Risk they share.

Communities that self-actuate carry their own risk. selfdriven.insure gives them the frameworks, intelligence, and channels to pool it among their members — and to see how it is priced, held, and paid out.

Members share the risk. The community carries on.
